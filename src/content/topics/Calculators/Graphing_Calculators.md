A graphing calculator (sometimes called a scientific calculator) is a digital type of calculator that has a screen capable of rendering functions that can be displayed as graphs. These calculators can be physical like a Texas Instruments TI-84 or digital like Desmos.com.

Graphing calculators are usually significantly more complex than four-function calculators in what they allow the user to compute with them.
