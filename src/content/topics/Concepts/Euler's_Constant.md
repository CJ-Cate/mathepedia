Euler's Constant is a famous value in mathematics known for its tendency to show up in the most unexpected of places. It famously shows up in a limit that shows the largest amount of compound interest you could receive in a single time period.
$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } \left( 1+\frac{1}{n} \right)^n
$$
