An integral is the opposite of the derivative. 

An integral is denoted by a extended 'S' with a function $f(x)$ and a chosen variable to integrate upon, usually $x$.


To integrate a function one must use an integral. If we are to integrate a function $f(x)$, the output is called the anti-derivative of $f$ and can be denoted with the capital-letter version of the function, $F(x)$. 

## Popular Integral notations

Integral notation is 

An integral can be either *definite*, when it has a lower-bound $a$ and upper bound $b$, or *indefinite* in which the bounds are absent.

This is an example of a definite integral:
$$
\int_{a}^{b}f(x)dx
$$
This is an example of an indefinite integral:
$$
\int f(x)dx
$$
