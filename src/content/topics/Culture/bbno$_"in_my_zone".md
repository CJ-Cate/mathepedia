Baby No Money (bbno$) is a Canadian hip-hop rapper. On August 18, 2025, he released a song alongside Valorant titled "in my zone". This song features the following line:
```
Ay, 3.1459, knock you out easy as pie
I'ma catch you out, catch you out, catch you out, catch you out, catch you out outisde
```
Unfortunately, this line is factually incorrect as the first 5 digits of pi are $3.1415$.
